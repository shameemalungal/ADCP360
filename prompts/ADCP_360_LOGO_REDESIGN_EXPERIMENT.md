# ADCP 360° Logo Redesign Experiment

## Purpose
Explore a logo variation that better suits the Wayfind-inspired dashboard visual language while preserving ADCP 360°'s veterinary and government identity.

## Status
Experimental only. The original official Brand Identity Board remains the controlling identity until the user approves a replacement.

## Design direction
- Contemporary, clean, bright and professional.
- Teal, turquoise, green and yellow gradients from the ADCP brand palette.
- Veterinary identity must remain recognisable.
- Maintain cattle and poultry symbolism, veterinary medical cross and optional syringe integrated wholly inside the circular emblem.
- Keep “ADCP 360°” highly legible.
- Avoid unrelated finance/commercial symbols and excessive detail.

## Required variants
1. Primary horizontal lockup with emblem and wordmark.
2. Compact sidebar logo.
3. Circular emblem for app icon/favicon.
4. Monochrome version for official documents.
5. Light and dark background variants.

## Restrictions
- Do not distort, crop or stretch the existing logo.
- Do not allow any emblem element to protrude outside its circular boundary.
- Maintain clear space.
- Keep original assets unchanged.
- Store experiments separately from approved brand assets.
- Review at large, sidebar and favicon sizes before approval.
