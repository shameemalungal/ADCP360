Product: Integrated Administration & Operations Platform
Organisation: Animal Disease Control Project, Malappuram, Kerala
Domain: adcpmalappuram.in
Design reference: ADCP 360° Brand Identity Board. Treat logos, palette, gradients, typography, imagery and component examples as the controlling visual direction.

ROLE
Act as a senior product designer and enterprise UX architect designing a production-quality government veterinary administration platform. Create a coherent responsive multi-screen web application UI prototype. This is an integrated operational system, not a marketing website. Prioritise clarity, fast data entry, traceability, readable tables, workflow status and operational dashboards.

PRODUCT CONTEXT
ADCP 360° centralises administration, inventory, finance, procurement, staff, vehicles, campaigns, vaccination, disease surveillance, sample collection, documents, reporting and digital signatures for ADCP Malappuram.
Guiding principle: Enter information once. Reuse it everywhere. Connect every department and every transaction. Generate reports automatically.
Web-first and responsive, with PWA-friendly layouts. Not offline-first.
Do not invent business rules beyond those defined. For pending rules, show a configurable setting or label “To be configured”.

BRAND SYSTEM
Use supplied ADCP 360° logo assets and variants. Do not redraw, distort, crop, recolour or invent another logo. Preserve the circular veterinary emblem and clear space. Use app-icon variant for favicon/app tile.
Palette: deep teal #0E7D7A; turquoise #14B8A6; nature green #84D46B; information blue #3B82F6; alert orange #F59E0B; highlight yellow #FCD34D; background #F0F9FF; surface #E2E8F0; text #475569.
Primary gradient #14B8A6 to #84D46B. Secondary gradient #3B82F6 to #06B6D4.
Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans. Bright light backgrounds, restrained shadows, subtle rounded cards, soft borders and clean spacing. Avoid dark-navy dominance.
Imagery: bright, natural Kerala landscape and healthy animals only where useful. Avoid decorative stock imagery in data-heavy screens.
Use a consistent modern icon family. Colour must not be the only status indicator; include text labels.

APPLICATION SHELL
Desktop: collapsible left sidebar with logo, product name and grouped navigation; top bar with breadcrumb, global search, notification bell, help, user avatar/name and role; main content with page title, contextual description and primary action.
Mobile: collapsible navigation drawer, compact top bar, horizontally scrollable tables or responsive cards, large touch targets.
Include reusable headers, filter bars, data tables, empty/loading/error states, dialogs, toasts and form validation.
Use clearly illustrative demo data, not actual live records. Use Indian conventions: ₹, DD/MM/YYYY and Indian number grouping.

NAVIGATION
Dashboard; Stock & Inventory; Finance & Utilisation Certificate; Purchase & Procurement; Staff Management; Vehicle Management; Office Administration; Document Management; Field Reporting & Form Builder; Campaign Management; Sample Collection Management; Disease Surveillance; Vaccination Reporting & Analysis; Reporting Centre; User & Access Management; Settings & Master Data; Digital Signature Service.

AUTHENTICATION
Login with email/password, Google sign-in, email OTP verification, forgot password and reset password.
OTP verification with masked email, code entry and resend state.
Admin-initiated password reset must not expose existing password and may force reset at next login.
Google login does not automatically authorise an account.

PROFILE AND MULTI-ROLE SWITCHING
Top-right profile dropdown: My Profile; Roles (when multiple role assignments exist); Account Settings; Change Password; Logout.
A single employee may hold multiple role/office assignments, including additional charge. Show role/designation, office/institution or jurisdiction, additional-charge indicator and currently active role. Switching roles keeps the same authenticated session, changes dashboard/modules/data scope based on assigned permissions and records an audit event. Do not create multiple user accounts for additional charges. If only one role exists, omit unnecessary role-switch UI.
Logout is functional and uses a confirmation dialog.

DASHBOARD
District dashboard with illustrative KPI cards: active campaigns, vaccination progress, pending completion reports, verification pending, current vaccine stock alerts, near-expiry items, monthly expenditure, pending UC, sample results pending and vehicle allocations due.
Charts: campaign progress, vaccination by species/vaccine, disease surveillance trend, expenditure by financial head.
Work queue: pending tasks, approvals, reports to receive and signatures required.
Alerts: low stock, expiry, vehicle renewals, maintenance/payment reminders.
Dashboard must respect role and scope.

ORGANISATION AND PERSONNEL
Organisation list for district/taluk/panchayath/municipality/institution/lab, hierarchy detail and add/edit.
Personnel list and profile: name, designation, office, Pashudhan ID, status, assignments and history.
Campaign assignment: person, campaign, squad, local body, role and effective dates. Support multiple assignments where authorised.

INVENTORY
Inventory dashboard with summaries, low-stock alerts, near-expiry alerts, pending issue notes and recent receipts.
Item master list/detail: category, unit/conversion, minimum vaccine stock, tracking settings, batch stock and ledger.
Goods receipt form: source, supplier, date, item, batch, quantity, unit, expiry and attachments.
Batch management.
Stock allocation planner: select item/batch, show available quantity, divide among multiple institutions, show allocation total and remaining quantity, record authorised officer and decision date. Allocation does not deduct stock.
Allocation details with revision history.
Issue note form. Pending note does not deduct stock.
Issue note detail with explicit “Completed Supply Successfully” action and confirmation that stock will be deducted only after completion.
Stock ledger, filters, returns/adjustments with reason/authorisation/audit.
Near-expiry list: item, category, batch, quantity, expiry, days remaining, storage location.
Low-stock vaccine alerts using configurable minimum threshold.
Institution advisory vaccine balance with four separate values: actual district stock, cumulative quantity supplied, estimated institutional balance and institution-reported physical stock. Explicitly label estimate as advisory, not actual stock.

FINANCE AND UC
Finance dashboard: financial head balances, receipts, expenditure, bank balance, cash in hand, unreconciled entries and UC status.
Financial heads master.
Transaction entry: receipt/expenditure/interest/bank charge/refund/adjustment, proceedings number/date, cheque details, encashment date and attachment.
Payment allocation: one payment split across multiple financial heads, retaining one payment record.
Head-wise ledger: opening, receipts, expenditure and running balance.
Bank statement PDF upload and extraction review: extracted date, narration, debit, credit, balance, proposed match, unmatched and mismatch states. User confirms matches; never auto-post extracted entries.
Bank reconciliation.
Cash advance: bank withdrawal transferred to cash balance; cash-in-hand under Office Expenses; cash expenses reduce advance balance. Prevent double counting of withdrawal as expenditure.
Cheque Issue Register exact column order: SL. NO. | BANK | CHQ NO. | DESCRIPTION/FOR | CHEQUE AMOUNT | CHEQUE PAYABLE DATE | REMARKS | PAYMENT STATUS. Include sample status PASSED; cancelled cheques remain, not deleted. Search, filter, print and export.
Monthly UC preserving official head-wise format, opening, receipts, available, expenditure, closing, total, cash in hand and bank balance. Do not invent official financial head labels or exact UC template if not supplied.

Vehicle master list/detail.

CAMPAIGN MANAGEMENT
Campaign dashboard: status, participating local bodies, squads, imports, completion reports and verification progress.
Campaign create/edit: name/type, dates, objectives, target population, orders/instructions, areas and sample schedule.
Squad form: normal Supervisor + Vaccinator + Helper; alternative Supervisor + two Vaccinators and no Helper. Include names/designations and Pashudhan IDs, campaign, taluk, Taluk Coordinator, local body, squad number, days allotted and assigned population.
Squad list allows multiple squads per local body and multiple assignments for a vaccinator as configured.
Squad review workflow: draft → Epidemiologist verification → Taluk/Supervisor suggestions → final publication.
Amendment screen with version history, old/new composition, reason, requester, approver, timestamp and effective date.
District/Taluk Crisis Management Committee membership.
Population master by local body and species.
Population Excel import: download template; upload; year/effective date; Full Update or Partial Update; validate; preview new/updated/error rows; duplicate detection; explicit confirmation; import history. Full Update replaces selected year's dataset only after confirmation. Partial Update changes only supplied species/local-body values.
Campaign population snapshot.
Bharath Pashudhan Excel import: upload, validate, map columns, detect duplicates, preview, import history, unallocated record queue.
Vaccination attribution: campaign + vaccinator Pashudhan ID + village mapped to local body + squad assignment. Ambiguous records require manual resolution; never duplicate.
Campaign performance dashboard with configurable campaign-specific formulas and thresholds. Mark “To be configured” until set; no universal formula.
Completion report register: campaign/local body, expected, received/pending, receipt date, receiver, source mode (email/online), uploaded copy and remarks.
Completion report form captures additional vaccinations not uploaded to Bharath Pashudhan by vaccine/species/count/reason/remarks.
Verification workflow: Supervisor → Taluk Coordinator → District Coordinator; separate receipt/review/signature states.
Verification sampling: Taluk Coordinator 5% and District Coordinator 2% of total vaccinations, independently recorded. Include total considered, sample count, selected records, actual verified count, findings, discrepancies and outcome. Sampling/rounding rule configurable/to be confirmed.
Verification report preview and signature status.
